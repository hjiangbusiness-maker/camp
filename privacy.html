RNDV Analytics Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

RNDV Analytics is an internal tool operated by RNDV. It reads YouTube Analytics data for YouTube channels whose owners have chosen to grant access through Google sign-in. It is not a public product and is used only by RNDV.

What we access

When a channel owner signs in, RNDV Analytics asks Google for read-only access:

  • YouTube Analytics (read-only): channel and video performance reports, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate.
  • YouTube Analytics monetary reports (read-only): estimated revenue for the channel's videos.
  • YouTube account (read-only): the channel's name, ID and its public video list.

The tool cannot upload, edit or delete videos, post comments, or change any setting. These reports are aggregated by YouTube; they do not identify individual viewers.

How we use it

Only to analyze the channel's performance and give the channel creative and strategic feedback, such as which thumbnails and video formats work best. We do not sell this data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.

How we store it

Reports are stored privately in RNDV's access-restricted systems. The sign-in token is kept on a single access-controlled machine and is never shared.

Google and YouTube policies

RNDV Analytics uses YouTube API Services. By granting access you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. RNDV Analytics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke RNDV Analytics' access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. To have the data we stored deleted, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.
