Message from Henry

I'm sure some of you have been wondering what's in store. We'll have an orientation day after everyone arrives at the house, but I can reveal a few general things now, along with a few important thoughts.

  1. Catch up on your sleep before you get here, and if you're crossing time zones, have a game plan for jet lag. We'll start our days like clockwork at 8:30 AM PST, and almost every evening will be left wide open for adventures, filming, and work sessions. I highly encourage everyone to take advantage of every moment.
  2. Run of show: Most days will contain 1-3 workshops, free time to film and create content, and a group recreational activity. You'll find our schedule is neither that long nor that rigid by design, but the spirit of this environment is to push ourselves together in this 10-day sandbox.
  3. A lot of you may want to catch up or meet new people while you're in town. My suggestion: instead of lining up brunches and coffee catchups, send those favorite people an invite to our party on the 12th. I want to encourage you to stay immersed with our core group of 10 during camp and channel your external social energy toward the end-of-camp dinner party. Each of you will be able to invite at least 2-3 people.
  4. A few of the bigger moments, without spoiling anything: a multi-day group creative challenge, a couple of full content days with light production support & crew, 5 creator speakers & 3 industry experts running workshops, and a dinner party on 8/12.

Over these 10 days, we'll be betting on ourselves and each other. You, your peers, RNDV, and the guest mentors are all converging from different walks of life to make this possible. This ecosystem is designed to be a melting pot: what can come out of 10 aspirational, ambitious, and hard working creators who come together to connect and grow?

I still won't reveal who's attending, but what we all have in common is that we're taking a risk to make what we enjoy our main endeavor. So bring your A-game and let's take steps toward making our wildest dreams come true.

One honest update: I tried hard to get us Head in the Clouds tickets for that Saturday and haven't been able to make it happen so far. TLDR a lot of people quit 88rising this year and it's a bit of a mess internally, so they pushed all ticketing to AEG. If things don't work out with that, we're going to make the most of that day another way, and I have some ideas :)

The place we'll be calling home is 2 minutes from Century City (very central).

Know before you land

  • Doors open Monday Aug 3 from 3pm. We're all having dinner together that first night.
  • Pickups run from LAX and BUR. If your flight gets delayed or plans change, just text me. Nobody is getting stranded.
  • Flying out: we recommend the morning of Aug 13. Evening of the 12th works too, just less ideal.
  • Any other questions: drop them in the Discord or text me.
